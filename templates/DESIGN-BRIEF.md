## Hard constraints
- Stack (HTML/CSS, React, Next, Tailwind, Svelte…):
- Brand assets that EXIST (logo, fonts, photos, product shots, video). Be honest; no asset → no fake asset.
- Asset slots to generate later (filled by the director in Step 4b): slot → size / length / mood
- Accessibility floor: WCAG AA, keyboard, reduced motion (default yes)
- Must support: dark mode? RTL? mobile first?

## Forbidden (add your own; these are the defaults)
- Inter / Arial / system-ui as display font
- Purple-blue gradients, glassmorphism glows, blobs
- Three-icon-card feature rows, nested cards, card-on-card
- Eyebrow labels, single-word colour accents in headlines
- Bounce / elastic easing, everything fading up on scroll with one duration
- Pure #000 / #fff, grey text on coloured backgrounds
- Stock "team in office" photography, emoji as icons
